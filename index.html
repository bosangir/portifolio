Research & software

Methods I build, and code you can run

My PhD at SCK CEN and Hasselt University develops statistical methods for integrating multi-omics data. Each method ships as open, reproducible software.

R packageMulti-omicsSimulation

SUMO

How do you test a multi-omics method when you never know the true signal in real data?

Simulation Utilities for Multi-Omics data. SUMO generates datasets with configurable latent factors, signal blocks, noise levels and heterogeneity, so methods for factor analysis, clustering and biclustering can be benchmarked against a known truth.

Ensemble learningIntegrative clustering

M-ABC

Can clusters that agree across several omics layers be trusted more than any single layer?

Multi-data Aggregating Bundles of Clusters extends the ABC method to joint clustering of multi-source data. It runs ensemble clustering within each dataset and integrates the sample-level dissimilarities into one consensus structure.

Which unsupervised factor method recovers biology best, and when does it fail?

A systematic comparison of unsupervised factor-analysis methods for multi-omics integration, using simulated truth from SUMO and real radiobiology data.

      Simulate before you trust

      If a method can't recover a known truth in simulation, it shouldn't be used on real patients.
